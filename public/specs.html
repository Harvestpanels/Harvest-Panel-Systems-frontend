FAQ

Quick Answers.

What Are Your Lead Times?
Stock panels ship within days, while custom orders usually take 3 to 8 weeks depending on specs and volume.
What Sizes Do Panels Come in?
Thickness ranges from 2" to 8" (51 to 203 mm; 4" / 100 mm is standard), lengths from 10' to 36' (3.0 to 11 m) with common stock lengths, and widths of 39.37" (1,000 mm) or 45" (1,143 mm). Custom sizes are also available.
How Much Do Panels Cost?
Prices typically run $4 to $8 per square foot ($43 to $86 per m²) based on length and thickness. A Best Price Guarantee matches a competitor's written quote, and a free estimate is available with your specs.
How Long Does Installation Take?
Installation can be an estimated 60 to 80% faster than traditional construction. A typical crew installs 1,000 to 1,500 square feet (93 to 139 m²) per day.
What Is the Insulation Value?
A 4" (100 mm) panel provides about R-30 (RSI 5.3), higher than the R-10 to R-19 typical of metal stud and drywall. Actual values depend on thickness and core material.
Can Panels Support Hanging Equipment?
Yes, with the right panel type and mounting hardware, they can hold hanging equipment and fixtures.
Do You Offer Installation?
Yes. Full installation is available, as well as supply-only orders.
How Are Panels Delivered?
Shipments leave the Oklahoma distribution center and reach anywhere in the U.S. within 48 hours of departure.
