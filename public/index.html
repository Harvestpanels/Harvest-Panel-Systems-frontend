Trim and accessories in a typical room

  • Floor track. 20 ga (0.91 mm) galvanized U-track, web sized to the panel thickness.
  • Inside corner trim. 2" × 2" × 26 ga (51 × 51 × 0.46 mm), continuous, on the room side.
  • Outside corner trim. Continuous 26 ga, on the warm side, over sealant.
  • Flat batten. 26 ga, 4" (102 mm) wide minimum, over a ceiling-to-ceiling butt joint above a partition.
  • Joint covers and Z-flashing. Where thickness changes or the drawings call for them.
  • Butyl and sealant. Under the track, at corners and joints, and on the warm side to keep the vapor seal continuous.

Project installation drawings and Harvest Panel Systems shop drawings always govern. See the trim and accessories.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can’t find an answer? Email Sales@harvestpanels.com.

Tell me about Harvest Panel Systems?
Harvest Panel Systems is a global distributor of Insulated Metal Panels and Doors, based in Piedmont, Oklahoma, and serving the cultivation, cold storage, data center, industrial, commercial, and residential markets with a modern, energy-efficient alternative to traditional construction.
What kind of services does Harvest Panel Systems offer?
We offer design assistance, budgeting, and free estimates on every Insulated Metal Panel and Door project, plus installation through our in-house crew and nationwide contractor partnerships.
Does Harvest Panel Systems provide free estimates?
Yes. Reach out through our contact form or by phone and our team will put together a no-cost estimate for your project.
Is Harvest Panel Systems licensed and insured?
Yes, Harvest Panel Systems is fully licensed and insured for panel and door installation projects nationwide.
Who uses Insulated Metal Panels?
Industrial and commercial builders, general contractors, and residential developers all rely on insulated metal panels for fast, energy-efficient interior spaces.
How are Panels constructed?
Panels are constructed from rigid foam insulation (such as PIR, PUR, or EPS) sandwiched between two metal facings, providing strength, insulation, and a clean finish in one system.
What are your lead times?
Stock panels ship within days. Custom orders are typically fulfilled within 3 to 8 weeks depending on spec and volume.
What are the benefits of using Panels?
Energy efficiency, fast installation, durability, and design flexibility, all in a single system that reduces labor and long-term maintenance costs.
Are Panels safe and easy to maintain?
Yes. Insulated metal panels are low-maintenance, moisture-resistant, and built to hold up over decades of use.
What products, other than Panels do you provide?
Alongside Insulated Metal Panels, we supply and install Insulated Metal Doors, plus the mounting hardware (unistrut, all-thread, C-channel) and trim (flashing, corner angles, floor track) to finish an install.
How much do Panels Cost?
Panels typically range from $4 to $8 per square foot, depending on length and thickness. We also offer a Best Price Guarantee, matching a competitor's written quote. Contact us with your specs for an accurate, free estimate.
What sizes do Panels come in?
Panel thickness ranges from 2" to 8" (51–203 mm; 4" / 100 mm is our standard), lengths run 10' to 36' (3.0–11 m; stock lengths of 10', 12', 14', 16', and 20'), and widths are 39.37" (1,000 mm) or 45" (1,143 mm). We can also manufacture custom sizes to fit your exact project dimensions.
Do you offer installation services?
Yes, we offer full installation services in addition to supply-only orders.
How long does it take to install Panels?
Installation can cut an estimated 60-80% off traditional construction time. A typical crew installs 1,000-1,500 sq ft (93-139 m²) a day, 5,000 sq ft (465 m²) in 4-5 days, and 10,000 sq ft (929 m²) in 7-10 days.
Insulation Value?
A 4" (100 mm) panel delivers an R-30 (RSI 5.3) insulation value, well above the R-10 to R-19 typical of metal stud and drywall construction. Exact values vary by thickness and core material, our team can recommend the right spec for your climate and use case.
Can Panels support hanging of equipment?
Yes, with the appropriate panel type and mounting hardware, panels can support hanging equipment and fixtures.
Do you offer financing on Panels?
Reach out to our sales team to discuss financing options available for your project.
How are Panels delivered?
Our centrally located Oklahoma-based distribution center ensures delivery anywhere in the U.S. within 48 hours after departure.
