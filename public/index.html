Trim and Accessories in a Typical Room

  • Floor Connection. Option A: 20 ga (0.91 mm) galvanized U-track, web sized to the panel thickness. Option B: two white HS aluminum L angles, 1.2 mm (16 ga), in 6 m (19′-8.22") lengths, one on each side of the panel. Either one is anchored to the slab and sealed with butyl; the project engineer sets the anchor spacing.
  • Screws. One screw type throughout: 1/4" × 1/4" × 7/8" (6.35 × 22 mm) Tek.
  • Inside Corner Trim. 2" × 2" × 26 ga (51 × 51 × 0.46 mm), continuous, on the room side.
  • Outside Corner Trim. Continuous 26 ga, on the warm side, over sealant.
  • Flat Batten. 26 ga, 4" (102 mm) wide minimum, over a ceiling-to-ceiling butt joint above a partition.
  • Joint Covers and Z-Flashing. Where thickness changes or the drawings call for them.
  • Ceiling Support. 3/8" (9.5 mm) galvanized all-thread hanger rods with hidden ceiling clips and carriage bolts, SL-01 aluminum ceiling tees on long spans, and unistrut across the bottom chords of roof trusses.
  • Butyl and Sealant. Under the track, at corners and joints, and on the warm side to keep the vapor seal continuous.

The Basics

What Is an Insulated Metal Panel (IMP)?

An Insulated Metal Panel (IMP) is a factory-made sandwich panel: two steel faces bonded to a rigid foam core (PIR, PUR, EPS or mineral wool). One panel is the wall or ceiling, the insulation and the finished surface.

R-30

Thermal Performance

A 4" (100 mm) panel delivers R-30 (RSI 5.3), well above the R-10 to R-19 typical of metal stud and drywall construction.

1 Panel

Wall, Insulation & Finish

Framing, insulation, vapor barrier, drywall and paint become one installed panel.

Fast

Installs in Days

A typical crew installs 1,000–1,500 sq ft (93–139 m²) a day, and 5,000 sq ft (465 m²) in 4–5 days.

Insulated Metal Panels (IMP) are used for cold storage and cold rooms, cultivation and grow rooms, cleanrooms, food processing, data centers, warehouses, aircraft hangars and pre-engineered metal buildings. See every industry.

What Are Insulated Metal Panels (IMP)?
Insulated Metal Panels (IMP) are factory-made sandwich panels with two steel faces bonded to a rigid foam core (PIR, PUR, EPS or mineral wool). They combine the wall or ceiling, the insulation and the finished surface in one panel, so rooms and buildings go up faster than with framing and drywall.
What Are Insulated Metal Panels (IMP) Used For?
Cold storage and cold rooms, walk-in coolers and freezers, cultivation and grow rooms, cleanrooms and laboratories, food and beverage processing, data centers, warehouses, aircraft hangars, workshops, modular buildings and pre-engineered metal buildings. See our industries page for details.
Can Insulated Metal Panels (IMP) Be Used for Cold Rooms and Walk-In Coolers?
Yes. Cold storage is one of the most common uses. Our 4" (100 mm) panel delivers R-30 (RSI 5.3), and we offer hygienic cold storage panels, hinged and sliding cooler doors, and the trim and sealants to finish a cold room. Tell us the temperature you need and our team will recommend the right panel.
Do You Offer Panels for Grow Rooms and Cultivation Facilities?
Yes. Insulated Metal Panels (IMP) help hold steady temperature and humidity for grow rooms, cultivation facilities and indoor farms, and the moisture-resistant steel and PVC faces wipe clean. Use the room calculator to size your room, then ask for a free estimate.
Can Insulated Metal Panels (IMP) Be Used for Hangars and Warehouses?
Yes. Insulated wall and roof panels are used on aircraft hangars, warehouses, distribution centers and pre-engineered metal buildings. Send us your spans, loads and local code requirements and we will recommend a wall or roof panel. Final engineering is by the project engineer.
